# Outlier Fitness | Bookings & Revenue Forecast

I built this Excel model to estimate potential revenue for Outlier Fitness, my personal training business. It shows how changes in weekly bookings and hourly rates affect projected revenue.

## Business question

How much revenue could Outlier Fitness generate at different booking levels and price points?

I charge $225 for an individual session and $200 per session when clients buy a 10-session package. Those rates give the forecast a basis in how the business actually prices its services.

## What this project demonstrates

- Building a revenue forecast in Excel from booking and pricing assumptions
- Comparing how session volume and package pricing affect projected revenue
- Applying financial modeling to a small business I own and operate
